Why Now

The AI unlock: personalized outreach at scale

At a €999 price point, traditional agency economics don't support high-volume, personalized outreach — researching, auditing, mocking up, and writing a pitch for each prospect manually is too labor-intensive to do at scale.

lead qualification → website crawl → 100-point audit → industry-tailored redesign → personalized outreach draft

A human stays in the loop for quality control and approval — but the heavy lifting happens automatically, at near-zero marginal cost. That turns “send each prospect a working preview of their new website” from a boutique service into a repeatable, scalable motion.
