## Slide 2 — The problem

Millions of local service businesses — dentists, plumbers, electricians,
lawyers, accountants, restaurants, salons, real estate agencies — are losing
customers to outdated websites.

Common issues:
- Outdated first impression
- Weak mobile experience
- Hidden contact options
- Unclear service messaging
- Low trust signals
- Slow or confusing layout

Owners know it, but: no time, no design skills, and traditional agencies are
slow (4–12 weeks) and expensive (€3,000–€10,000+). Meanwhile, every visitor
who bounces goes straight to a competitor with a better site.

---

## Slide 3 — The solution

**We Analyze** — an AI-powered audit scores the business's current website
against a standardized 100-point conversion and UX framework, and pinpoints
exactly what's costing them customers.

**We Design** — we generate a modern MVP homepage concept, tailored to their
industry, business, and customers.

**We Elevate** — fast, affordable launch support gets a professional,
conversion-focused website live.

The key difference: the prospect *sees their actual new homepage* — a live,
private preview — before they pay anything.

---

## Slide 4 — How it works

1. **Analyze** — standardized AI-powered audit against the 100-point
   framework.
2. **Design** — modern MVP homepage concept, tailored to industry and brand.
3. **Build** — once approved, the real MVP is built: responsive layout,
   contact form, basic SEO.
4. **Launch** — live and hosting-ready within 48 hours of approval.

Nothing is built or sent without the business owner's review and approval at
every step.

---

## Slide 5 — Why this works now (the AI unlock)

At a €999 price point, traditional agency economics don't support
high-volume, personalized outreach — researching, auditing, mocking up, and
writing a pitch for each prospect manually is too labor-intensive to do at
scale.

WLABS automates the labor-intensive parts with an internal AI agent
pipeline:

`lead qualification → website crawl → 100-point audit → industry-tailored redesign → personalized outreach draft`

A human stays in the loop for quality control and approval — but the heavy
lifting (research, design draft, audit, copywriting) happens automatically
and at near-zero marginal cost. That turns "send each prospect a working
preview of their new website" from a boutique service into a repeatable,
scalable motion.

## Slide 9 — Go-to-market

The product *is* the outreach engine. The audit identifies who needs help
and how badly (an "opportunity score"), and the platform builds the pitch
automatically — including a working preview of the prospect's new site.

Every outreach email includes:
- The specific issues found on their current site
- A link to their private, AI-generated preview
- Transparent, fixed pricing
- A clear opt-out, with suppression honored across the whole pipeline

Plan: land first customers in 1–2 verticals/regions, use the results as case
studies and testimonials for the next wave of outreach.
